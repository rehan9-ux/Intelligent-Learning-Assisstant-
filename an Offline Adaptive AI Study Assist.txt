The core idea is to build a system where students can upload their own study materials (PDFs, notes, textbooks) and receive personalized tutoring, auto-generated quizzes, and adaptive explanations — all without requiring an internet connection.

The key outcomes of this project are a self-improving AI tutor that personalizes explanations based on individual performance, works fully offline with no cloud dependency, and demonstrates practical integration of RAG, local LLMs, and classical ML in a single system.
